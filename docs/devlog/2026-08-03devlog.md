Started with config.py, the sys.path bridge to SEEKERS_GHOSTS. Wrote the sentinel check, the three gates, ran a quick test to confirm they were distinct objects. ModuleNotFoundError: No module named 'app.concurrency'.

Had actually flagged this exact risk months ago in INSTRUCTIONS.md and it turned out not to be hypothetical. The moment anything does `from app import config`, Python caches "app" in sys.modules as this project's own package, since that's what's on sys.path first. By the time config.py runs its own sys.path insert, it's too late.
